    Data: anonymized consumer loan dataset (38,480 records). Cleaning: removed 2 invalid rows (zero loan amount, issue date in 1999). A loan counts as a default when it was charged off, in default or more than 15 days late. Rates for groups with fewer than 100 loans are flagged because small samples are unreliable.
